# 🎮 CretCollect  

**CretCollect** is a free and open-source application to manage and track your **video game collection**.  
Whether you’re into retro classics or modern titles, CretCollect helps you keep everything organized in one place.  

Built with [CretNet.Platform](https://github.com/yveslaurentcreton/CretNet), it’s designed to be **modern, flexible, and community-driven**.  

## ✨ Features (planned & in progress)  

- 📂 **Track your games** – organize your video game collection with ease  
- 🔎 **Search & browse** – quickly find titles in your library  
- 🎨 **User-friendly interface** – clean and simple design for collectors  
- � **Containerized deployment** – easy setup with Docker for seamless installation  
- 🏠 **Self-hosting ready** – run on your own server for complete control and privacy  
- �🔧 **Extensible foundation** – powered by CretNet.Platform, easy to grow with new features  
- 👐 **Open-source** – free for everyone, built by the community  

## 🎯 Project Goal  

The goal of **CretCollect** is to provide gamers with a **free, transparent, and open alternative** for cataloging their video game libraries — without being locked into commercial software. With containerized deployment and self-hosting capabilities, you maintain full control over your data and privacy.
